# The statistical theory of phylogenetic inference, in Lean

[![CI](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-statistics/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-statistics/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Lean 4 formalizations of the statistical theory of phylogenetic inference, one paper per repository:
what methods can and cannot achieve from finite data. They start with fast convergence and the
sequence lengths that the reconstruction of trees needs; continue with statistical consistency and
its failure when the model is wrong; then the discordance of gene trees and the limits of
species-tree inference, and the identifiability and consistency of indel models; and end with the
asymptotics of tests near singularities and boundaries.

This repository is their hub, in the collection rooted at
[lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics). It holds:

- the library [`PhyloStatistics`](PhyloStatistics), for what two papers or more of this hub share and the root library
  does not hold. It has nothing yet: what they share so far (trees and tree metrics, the coalescents and the multispecies coalescent) is in the root library.
  Its [README](PhyloStatistics/README.md) says what goes there;
- the catalog of the papers, [`catalog/papers.toml`](catalog/papers.toml), from which the tables
  below are generated.

The [conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md) that
every repository follows, the [workflow](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/WORKFLOW.md),
the registry of the repositories, the shared foundations and the tools are in the root. Each paper is
formalized with the [formalize-math-paper](https://github.com/vltanh/formalize-math-paper) skill:
every result the paper proves is proved by the paper's own argument, the statements are checked
against the paper, and an audit of the paper reports its errors and gaps.

## The papers

Each paper is in one family, the one its question belongs to. This is a hub of theory: general
results on consistency, sample complexity and asymptotics are here, and a result about one estimator
is with that estimator, in a hub of inference
([conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md), Section
1): the sample complexity of ASTRAL is with ASTRAL, in
[lean4-phylo-species-tree](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-species-tree). *Uses* lists the
papers, of any hub, that the paper's proofs are known to use, and the other hubs whose library it
requires. Each paper's inventory completes the list.

## Starting a paper

From [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics), beside this repository,
when the paper's formalization starts:

```sh
python3 scripts/status.py next           # the open papers, by priority, with what each waits on
python3 scripts/new_paper.py mossel2004  # creates ../lean4-mossel2004 on the train, under this hub
cd ../lean4-mossel2004 && claude         # then: /formalize-math-paper https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-03-03382-8
```

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake build Phylogenetics
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

Lean and Mathlib `v4.37.0-rc1`; [`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml) requires lean4-phylogenetics by
commit, `3fcabfd`. The audit, [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean), checks that every
declaration of this library and of lean4-phylogenetics depends only on Lean's standard axioms
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; `python3 scripts/gen_audit.py` generates its
`import all` lines. CI runs the build and the audit, and checks that the tables above are current.
